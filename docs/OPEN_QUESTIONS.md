# Open questions for the client

Points the specification leaves open or contradicts. For each one we picked a default and
built it, so work can continue. **Every default below is in use now.** We will confirm
them with the client once they have tested the MVP.

How to add one: put it under its area, give it the next number, and write it in plain
English. Say what we do now, and the reason in a few words if that helps. When the client
answers, write the answer and the date under the question, and change the code if the
answer differs from the default.

## Leadership conversations

From [KEY_PERSON_DISCOVERY.md](KEY_PERSON_DISCOVERY.md).

**LC-1. Does pricing need to wait for every manager, or only for the main contact?**
*Now:* pricing starts once the main contact has spoken to us. Other managers can keep
going, and anyone they add after that is marked as added after pricing.

**LC-2. When should other managers be invited?**
*Now:* as soon as the company finishes onboarding, not after the main contact's
conversation.

**LC-3. Do managers need an account with a login?**
*Now:* no. They get a private link.

**LC-4. How can managers answer?**
*Now:* by typing or sending voice notes. Live spoken conversation waits until we choose a
voice provider.

**LC-5. Should a manager get a one-time code when they open their link on a new device?**
*Now:* no.

**LC-6. Which AI model should run these conversations for real clients?**
*Now:* not decided. We need this before release.

**LC-7. Is it fine to use the agreed conversation instructions word for word?**
Their first paragraph was written for staff, not managers.
*Now:* we use them as written.

**LC-8. Can a short, neutral summary of what leadership said reach staff interviews
without a consultant checking it first?**
*Now:* yes. An automatic check makes sure nothing private or critical gets through.

**LC-9. How long do we keep conversation transcripts?**
*Now:* we tell people we keep them "while Mjadi works with your company". The real policy
needs confirming.

## Stage 1 pricing

From [STAGE_1_PRICING.md](STAGE_1_PRICING.md).

**PR-1. What starts Stage 1?**
*Now:* the client accepting the quote. One document only mentions our approval and
sending the quote.

**PR-2. When are staff invited?**
*Now:* after the client accepts the quote.

**PR-3. What are the real prices?**
*Now:* the rate card holds placeholder figures, and the admin screens say so.

**PR-4. Is a separate approval step needed before a quote is sent?**
*Now:* approving a quote sends it. We always record who approved it and when.

**PR-5. Which reasons can be given for changing a price by hand?**
*Now:* the longer list in the pricing document, including "bundle".

**PR-6. How is VAT charged?**
*Now:* 5% for the UAE. Another country needs its rate added first. Our margin is
worked out before tax.

**PR-7. How many deep dives does a quote include by default?**
*Now:* one per participant, but never fewer than 3 or more than 5. An administrator can
change it.

**PR-8. Should managers' conversations be charged for?**
*Now:* they count towards our hours and cost, but have no price line of their own.
Managers added after pricing has started are noted but not charged.

**PR-9. How do we charge for extra staff?**
*Now:* we count people, we don't track names. Only people beyond the accepted number are
extra, so swapping one person for another costs nothing.

**PR-10. What do we compare a new quote with?**
*Now:* accepted Stage 1 quotes of a similar size, marked "actual hours not recorded yet".

**PR-11. Who delivers Stage 1?**
*Now:* consultants only. Changing the mix of people waits for Stage 2.

**PR-12. What are the payment terms and the wording of the engagement terms?**
*Now:* placeholder text on the quote. Nothing is paid before staff are invited.

**PR-13. Do quotes need to be in Arabic?**
*Now:* English only. The app has no translation yet.

**PR-14. Who can change the plan once a quote exists?**
*Now:* Mjadi can, until the client accepts. Each change makes a new quote. The client
can't change it.

**PR-15. How is an engagement cancelled before Stage 1?**
*Now:* only Mjadi can close it, and must give a reason.

**PR-16. What happens if the consultant later picks more deep dives than the quote
included?**
*Now:* nothing yet. We keep the number included so the team choosing deep dives can be
warned.

**PR-17. Can consultants see our internal figures, such as cost and margin?**
*Now:* no. They see what the client sees.

## Bringing staff and leadership findings together

From [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md).

**SY-1. Which list of categories should we use?**
The data document and the synthesis document use slightly different lists for the same
thing. For example, one says "System" and the other "Systems", and one has "Organisation"
where the other has "Change".
*Now:* we use the synthesis document's list, because it is newer and more detailed.

**SY-2. How should we decide which opportunities matter most?**
We keep two separate rankings: what management told us is important, and our own ranking
from the evidence. The specification doesn't say how our own ranking should work.
*Now:* an opportunity ranks higher when it frees up more staff time, affects more
departments, rests on strong evidence, or when staff confirm something management also
raised.

**SY-3. When management and staff see something differently, how do we name management in
the report?**
For example, management may say "finance is slow" while staff describe approvals as the
real hold-up. The report is often passed on to others.
*Now:* we write "Management reports…" and never name the person or their job title (such
as "the CEO").

**SY-4. How much needs to be covered before these combined findings can go into a report?**
The specification says a report needs no minimum, as long as it says what was covered.
*Now:* we include whatever we have and say clearly which departments haven't been covered
yet.

**SY-5. Where should the bigger, company-level opportunities appear in the report?**
Some findings are bigger than one person's task, such as "prices are kept in three places
across three departments".
*Now:* they appear in a short section inside the findings, "What connects the findings".
They are not yet items the client can pick for the next stage.

**SY-6. How should we judge how advanced a company already is with technology and AI?**
The specification asks us to rate each company as Foundational, Developing or Advanced
using a fixed rule rather than the company's own opinion, but doesn't give the rule.
*Now:* not built. We need a description of each level, or the signs to look for.

**SY-7. If a consultant corrects the hours for work several people share, should the
report totals change?**
*Now:* no. The correction is used when choosing deep dives, but the report keeps the
figures each person gave us. Hours can still be corrected finding by finding, as before.
