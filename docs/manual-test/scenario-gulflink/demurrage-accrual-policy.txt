GulfLink Logistics LLC — Demurrage & Detention Accrual Policy
Document ID: GL-OPS-DEM-2026-03 | Effective: 2026-03-01 | Owner: Ops Finance / Customs Desk
Sites: Jebel Ali Free Zone, Dubai HQ, Abu Dhabi bonded warehouse

1. SCOPE
This policy covers ocean demurrage, detention, storage, and inspection-hold charges arising from
customs clearance delays, short-ship POD exceptions, and carrier free-time breaches. Accruals must
hit the correct SAP GL month even when the formal customs clearance note arrives late.

2. SYSTEMS OF RECORD
- CargoWise TMS: free-time clocks, container events, exception IDs
- SAP S/4HANA: GL accruals (freight opex), FB60 vendor invoices
- Excel demurrage tracker: interim worksheet owned by Ops Finance (copy-paste from CargoWise exports)
- SharePoint: carrier debit notes and customs correspondence
- WhatsApp Business: warehouse ↔ customs ↔ AP exception triage

3. ACCRUAL RULES
A. Same-month accrual is mandatory when:
   - Container free time expired in-month, OR
   - Customs inspection hold exceeded 48 hours, OR
   - POD shows short-ship / damaged carton that will trigger carrier claim
B. If customs note is missing at month-end, Ops Finance books a provisional accrual using:
   - CargoWise estimated demurrage rate × calendar days over free time
   - Dual approval in Excel tracker (Ops Finance + Finance Controller) above AED 10,000
C. True-up in the following month when carrier PDF arrives; unexplained variance > AED 2,000
   requires CFO note in the month-end close pack.

4. FRICTION POINTS (DOCUMENTED)
- Manual spreadsheet: daily CargoWise CSV paste into Excel demurrage tracker (~2 hours/day).
- Approval bottlenecks: provisional accruals over AED 25,000 wait on CFO; average 4 days.
- Data silos: TMS events not integrated to SAP; HubSpot tickets for customer chargebacks lag Excel.
- Handwritten POD notes force retyping before demurrage claim packages are assembled.
- Weekend vessel cut-offs create Monday backlog; WhatsApp threads lose attachment lineage.

5. WORKFLOW
1) Warehouse flags exception on POD (photo to WhatsApp + SharePoint).
2) Customs desk updates CargoWise exception ID.
3) Ops Finance updates Excel demurrage tracker and proposes accrual.
4) Finance Controller reviews vs AP approval matrix thresholds.
5) SAP posting or provisional journal; AP releases vendor invoice when PDF matches tracker.

6. KPI TARGETS
- Same-month demurrage accrual coverage: ≥ 95%
- Excel↔SAP reconciliation variance: < 2% of monthly demurrage spend
- Exception aging > 5 days: < 10% of open demurrage lines
- Average CFO wait on provisional accrual > AED 25k: ≤ 3 business days
