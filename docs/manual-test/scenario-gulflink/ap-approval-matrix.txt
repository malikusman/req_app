GulfLink Logistics LLC — Accounts Payable Approval Matrix
Document ID: GL-FIN-AP-2026-01 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Currency: AED | Owner: Finance Controller
Location: Dubai HQ / Jebel Ali Free Zone operations | Review cycle: Quarterly

PURPOSE
Vendor payments, freight-cost settlements, demurrage invoices, and warehouse opex follow this matrix.
When TMS (CargoWise) and SAP S/4HANA are out of sync, AP clerks still use a manual Excel AP workbook
and copy-paste invoice lines from carrier PDFs. This re-entry is slow, error-prone, and creates
approval bottlenecks when the CFO is travelling. SharePoint AP folder is the interim landing zone
before SAP FB60 posting. WhatsApp Business is used for exception triage between warehouse and finance.

APPROVAL THRESHOLDS (AED)
Threshold (AED)     | Approver              | System of record                         | SLA
0 – 5,000           | AP Clerk              | Excel AP workbook + Outlook email        | Same day
5,001 – 25,000      | Finance Controller    | SAP FB60 + SharePoint AP folder          | 2 business days
25,001 – 100,000    | CFO                   | Dual approval in SAP + wet-ink backup    | 5 business days
100,001+            | CEO                   | Board memo + SAP release                 | 10 business days

SPOT-RATE AND EXCEPTION LANES
- Spot-rate ocean freight debit notes: always Finance Controller minimum, even if under AED 5,000.
- Carrier claims / short-ship adjustments: attach POD scan + CargoWise exception ID before Excel entry.
- Demurrage / detention at Jebel Ali: treat as opex; if accrual > AED 25,000 escalate to CFO same day.
- Intercompany recharges (GCC hubs): dual GL coding in SAP required; Excel tracker must show lane code.

KNOWN FRICTION
1. Manual data entry: AP clerks re-enter carrier invoices into SAP after checking PDF and scanned PODs.
2. Approval bottlenecks: invoices over 25,000 AED queue for CFO sign-off; average wait is 4–7 days.
3. Data silos: TMS lane costs (CargoWise) do not reconcile automatically with Excel opex or SAP GL.
4. Spreadsheet dependency: month-end close pack is assembled by repetitive copy-paste from the warehouse
   cost sheet into Excel. Controllers spend 15–40 hours/month matching duplicates.
5. Coordination overhead: email and WhatsApp handoffs between warehouse, customs, and finance delay
   exception resolution (damaged carton / short ship notes on PODs).
6. PO matching failures: roughly 1 in 5 freight invoices fail three-way match (PO / GRN / invoice) and
   land in the Excel exception tab for 3–12 days before SAP release.
7. Duplicate payments risk: same carrier PDF sometimes uploaded twice to SharePoint without hash check.

EXCEPTIONS
- Demurrage and storage invoices at Jebel Ali often exceed 25,000 AED and require CFO dual approval.
- Spot-rate freight billing exceptions must attach the freight-billing SOP checklist before SAP posting.
- Handwritten POD exceptions must be typed into Excel before payment release (OCR not yet trusted).
- Customs fine / inspection holds: Finance must accrue in the same month if clearance note is delayed.
- Weekend vessel cut-offs: AP clerks may pre-stage Excel lines but must not post SAP until dual approval.

CONTROLS
- Dual approval above 5,000 AED is mandatory (no single-person release).
- Wet-ink backup retained for audits (ISO 27001 / local data residency).
- Quarterly review of Excel trackers vs SAP postings by Finance Controller.
- Monthly sample of 25 POD exceptions reviewed for handwriting → Excel transcription accuracy.
- Segregation of duties: AP Clerk who enters Excel cannot be sole SAP releaser above AED 5,000.
