GulfLink Logistics LLC — Customer Freight SLA & Exception FAQ (Internal)
Document ID: GL-CS-SLA-2026-02 | Audience: Customer Success + Finance AP | Region: UAE / GCC

Q1. What is the standard AP cycle commitment to carriers?
A. Target 8 calendar days invoice-to-pay. Actual Q1 performance is 11–14 days when PO matching fails
   or CFO dual approval is required above AED 25,000 (see AP approval matrix).

Q2. Why do customers see delayed debit-note responses?
A. HubSpot tickets wait on Excel exception-tab resolution. AP retypes POD handwriting into Excel
   before SAP FB60. Average exception aging is 3–12 days (~1 in 5 invoices fail three-way match).

Q3. How are demurrage disputes handled?
A. Ops Finance uses CargoWise free-time clocks + Excel demurrage tracker. Same-month accrual is
   required even if customs notes are late. Customer chargebacks sync to HubSpot after Finance
   Controller sign-off.

Q4. Which tools are involved in exception triage?
A. CargoWise TMS, SAP S/4HANA, Excel AP workbook, SharePoint AP folder, Outlook, WhatsApp Business,
   Microsoft Teams (close war-room), HubSpot.

Q5. What are the top bottlenecks Customer Success should set expectations around?
A. (1) Manual spreadsheet re-entry from carrier PDFs and POD scans
   (2) Approval bottlenecks for invoices / demurrage over AED 25,000
   (3) Data silos between TMS lane costs and SAP GL
   (4) Coordination overhead across warehouse, customs, and finance via email/WhatsApp

Q6. Is OCR trusted for POD exceptions?
A. Not yet. Handwritten POD exceptions must be typed into Excel before payment release.

Q7. What improvement is the AI pilot targeting?
A. Document extraction for POD/invoice lines, exception triage automation, and workflow automation
   so Excel dependency and dual-approval wait times drop within a 3–6 month horizon.
