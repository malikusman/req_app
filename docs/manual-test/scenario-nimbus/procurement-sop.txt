Nimbus Trading Co LLC — Procurement & Purchase Order SOP
Document ID: NB-PROC-SOP-2026-01 | Effective: 2026-01-15 | Owner: Procurement Manager
Sites: Dubai HQ, Jebel Ali warehouse

1. PURPOSE
Governs supplier selection, purchase orders (LPOs), and proforma invoice (PI) checking for
imported goods (electronics, home appliances). When a sales order is confirmed, Procurement
raises an LPO and requests a PI from the supplier before any payment.

2. SYSTEMS OF RECORD
- Zoho Inventory: stock levels, purchase orders (LPO numbers)
- Excel PO tracker: interim worksheet owned by Procurement (copy-paste from Zoho exports)
- Outlook: supplier quotes, PIs, and shipping confirmations by email
- WhatsApp Business: supplier follow-ups and freight updates

3. PI CHECKING (the core friction)
A. Every PI is checked MANUALLY against: the LPO raised, the emailed quote (prices), and the
   agreed Incoterms and payment terms. There is no automated match.
B. Roughly 1 in 4 PIs has a mismatch (price, quantity, or shipping term) versus the LPO.
C. A procurement officer spends about 20-30 minutes per PI reconciling by hand across Outlook,
   Zoho, and the Excel tracker.
D. Target PI-to-payment approval time is 2 days; actual is 4-6 days when a mismatch is found.

4. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS (AED)
0 - 10,000        | Procurement Officer   | Zoho + email        | 1 day
10,001 - 50,000   | Procurement Manager   | Zoho + Excel + email | 2 business days
50,001+           | Finance Director      | dual sign-off        | 4 business days
