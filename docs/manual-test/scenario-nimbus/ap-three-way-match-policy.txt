Nimbus Trading Co LLC — Accounts Payable Three-Way Match Policy
Document ID: NB-FIN-AP-2026-02 | Effective: 2026-02-01 | Currency: AED | Owner: Finance Director

PURPOSE
Supplier invoices are paid only after a three-way match: purchase order (LPO) + goods receipt
note (GRN) + supplier invoice. AP re-keys invoice lines from PDF invoices into the accounting
system because Zoho Inventory and the accounting package are not integrated.

CURRENT-STATE KPIs
Metric                                | Target   | Current
Invoice-to-pay cycle                  | 5 days   | 9-12 days
Three-way match first-pass rate       | 95%      | ~72%
Exception aging (unmatched > 5 days)  | < 10%    | ~30%
Manual re-keying per invoice          | 0        | 100% (every invoice)

FRICTIONS
- AP clerks copy-paste invoice numbers, amounts, and vendor codes between the PDF, Excel, and the
  accounting system daily — slow and error-prone.
- When the GRN is missing (logistics hasn't posted receipt), the invoice parks in an Excel
  exception tab and ages 5-14 days.
- Month-end close is a crunch; accruals must hit the right period even when supplier paperwork
  arrives late.
