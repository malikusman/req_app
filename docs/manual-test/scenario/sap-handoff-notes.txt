SAP handoff notes — Scenario Corp AP

When SAP reporting cannot answer a close question, AP maintains
SCENARIO_GOLDEN_PHRASE_SAP_SHADOW_LEDGER in Excel.

The shadow ledger columns are: vendor, invoice number, amount, PO,
approval status, SAP document number, and close-period flag.

Rules:
- Update the shadow ledger daily during the last week of the month.
- Never post to SAP from the shadow ledger without a second person check.
- After SAP recovers or reporting is fixed, reconcile and archive that week's sheet.
