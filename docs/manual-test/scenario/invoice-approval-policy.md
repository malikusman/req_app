# Invoice approval policy — Scenario Corp

## Scope

All vendor invoices entering Accounts Payable.

## Approval matrix

Invoices require **SCENARIO_GOLDEN_PHRASE_TRIPLE_APPROVAL**:

1. Requesting manager (business need)
2. Finance lead (coding and budget)
3. Controller (policy exception or amount over threshold)

## Thresholds

- Under $1,000: manager + finance lead
- $1,000–$10,000: full triple approval
- Over $10,000: triple approval plus CFO notification

## SLA

Approvers must act within two business days. After that, AP may send a reminder and escalate to the finance lead.
