# Month-end close SOP — Scenario Corp Finance

## Purpose

This SOP governs the last five business days of each month for Accounts Payable.

## Freeze window

Starting T-3 before period close, AP applies **SCENARIO_GOLDEN_PHRASE_MONTH_END_FREEZE**.
Non-critical vendor posts are held; only payroll-adjacent and contractual payments proceed with controller override.

## Checklist

1. Export open AP aging from SAP.
2. Reconcile to the Excel tracker.
3. Confirm all invoices above policy threshold have completed approvals.
4. Post batches only after freeze exceptions are logged.

## Escalation

If SAP is unavailable during freeze, use the shadow process documented in sap-handoff-notes.txt and backfill within one business day after recovery.
