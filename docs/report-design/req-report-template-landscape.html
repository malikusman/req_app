01 — Executive summary

The story in one read

With three of four invited employees completing a discovery interview, Acme Corp reached a readiness score of 75 — high enough to surface a clear, actionable picture of how work really happens across Finance and Operations.

The strongest recurring theme is manual, spreadsheet-bound process work in Finance, echoed by tool-dependency and duplicate-entry signals in Operations. These cluster into three cross-team patterns with concrete, catalog-matched fixes.

This report is the versioned source of truth for those findings. Every figure below is drawn from the discovery snapshot and has passed expert review before delivery.

Manual reconciliation in spreadsheets
Strength 0.82

Month-end close depends on hand-built spreadsheets passed between three analysts, creating rework, version conflicts, and multi-day delays.

“I rebuild the same sheet every month from scratch.” — Finance analyst
Tooling · Operations
Tool dependency on a single admin
Strength 0.68

Only one person can configure the scheduling tool, so the whole ops calendar stalls whenever they are unavailable.

“If Sam's out, scheduling just waits.” — Operations lead

Approval bottlenecks
Strength 0.61

“Small approvals sit for a week.” — Team member

Duplicate data entry across systems
Strength 0.58

Cross-team themes

07 — Recommendations

Suggested actions

High priority

Automate month-end reconciliation

Replace hand-built close spreadsheets with a templated, validated workflow.

Catalog match: Finance Close Automation
High priority

Cross-train ops tooling ownership

Document and share scheduling-tool configuration beyond a single admin.

Catalog match: Ops Resilience Playbook
Medium priority

Consolidate duplicate data entry

Integrate the three order systems so details are entered once.

Catalog match: Systems Integration Assessment
Medium priority

Delegate routine approvals

Auto-route approvals below a set threshold to team leads.

Catalog match: Approval Policy Redesign
Medium priority

Standardize KPI definitions

Agree one shared definition per metric across teams.

Catalog match: Metric Governance Kit
Low priority

Capture tribal knowledge

Build a living knowledge base for critical, undocumented steps.
